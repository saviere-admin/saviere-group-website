Developing

Tooth Dots.

The Engineering Context

  • — Conventional oral-care products are transported with substantial water content.
  • — Traditional tube packaging generates ongoing material and waste challenges globally.
  • — Solid-format oral care provides opportunities for more efficient packaging and logistics.

Current Investigation

Savière is currently focused on the development of a waterless oral care solution. Our ongoing research and engineering efforts are evaluating waterless formulation, solid dosage formats, moisture stability, and packaging efficiency.

Note: The Tooth Dots product is currently under development and is not yet commercially available.
