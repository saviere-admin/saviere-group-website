The Dots Company.

Replacing plastic silica paste tubes with clinical-grade, zero-water biomimetic Nano-HAp solid-state tablets. Scroll to view the clinical pipeline.

1. The Clinical Chemistry

The formulation requires biomimetic Nano-Hydroxyapatite (Nano-HAp) to rebuild enamel, Xylitol to selectively starve pathogenic bacteria, and Zinc Citrate to neutralize volatile sulfur compounds.

2. Anhydrous Compression

Water is eliminated. The active ingredients are pressed into a precise 600mg solid-state tablet format, slashing global shipping carbon emissions by 70%.

3. Zero-Plastic Vaults

Tablets drop into sustainable, refillable brushed aluminum vessels. This achieves absolute zero plastic contact, preventing daily microplastic ingestion linked to metabolic syndrome.
