Phase 1 Operations

The Dots Company.

Replacing plastic silica paste with clinical-grade, zero-water biomimetic Nano-HAp tablets.

1. The Clinical Chemistry

The formulation requires biomimetic Nano-Hydroxyapatite (Nano-HAp) to rebuild enamel, Xylitol to selectively starve pathogenic bacteria, and Zinc Citrate to neutralize volatile sulfur compounds.

2. Anhydrous Compression

Water is eliminated. The active ingredients are pressed into precise 600mg solid-state tablets, slashing global shipping carbon emissions by 70%.

3. Airtight Glass Vaults

Tablets drop into transparent glass vessels sealed with a crimson silicone gasket. This achieves absolute zero plastic contact, preventing daily microplastic ingestion.
