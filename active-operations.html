The Context

Our current investigation focuses on the packaging inefficiencies, moisture stability, and logistical footprint of conventional oral care. We are examining how transitioning away from liquid-heavy formats could potentially streamline logistics and reduce consumer material waste.

Areas of Investigation:

  • Waterless formulation stability
  • Packaging reduction & efficiency
  • Product portability
  • Supply chain logistics

Development Process

01

Research & Formulate

Reviewing literature and designing solid-format compositions.

02

Test & Validate

Ensuring moisture stability, performance, and baseline safety thresholds.

03

Scale

Establishing manufacturing capability prior to commercial launch.
