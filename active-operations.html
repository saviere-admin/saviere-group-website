FMCG Research.

To combat the 1.5 billion plastic tubes entering Indian landfills, Savière Group is actively architecting anhydrous (waterless) FMCG protocols.

The Anhydrous Mandate

By abstracting water out of the manufacturing process, we eliminate the need for pollutive multi-laminate plastics entirely. Clinical-grade active ingredients are instead secured in 100% recyclable architectural glass vaults.
