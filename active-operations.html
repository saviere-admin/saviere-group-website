Phase 1 Operations

The Dots Company.

Replacing pollutive silica plastic tubes with clinical-grade, zero-water biomimetic Nano-HAp solid-state vaults.

1. Biomimetic Chemistry

Synthesizing clinical-grade Nano-Hydroxyapatite (Nano-HAp) to rebuild human enamel without the neurotoxic risks of groundwater fluorosis.

2. Anhydrous Compression

By eliminating the 50% dead-weight water found in traditional tubes, we compress formulas into 600mg tablets, slashing freight carbon emissions by up to 70% globally.

3. Infinite Glass Vaults

Discarding the 1.5 billion plastic tubes landfilled in India annually. Tablets are securely deposited into endlessly recyclable, clear architectural glass.

4. Absolute Preservation

A heavy, brushed-aluminum lid seals the micro-environment, ensuring long-term structural integrity and zero plastic leaching in humid environments.
