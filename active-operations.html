Phase 1 Intervention

FMCG Healthcare Engine.

An engineered, zero-waste intervention designed to replace highly pollutive, water-heavy synthetics with sustainable, earth-safe alternatives.

The Engineering Context

  • — Conventional consumer products are transported with substantial dead-weight water content.
  • — Traditional tube packaging generates ongoing material and micro-plastic waste challenges globally.
  • — Solid-format architectures provide massive opportunities for more efficient packaging and logistics.

Strategic Execution

Savière Group is currently in active research and development of highly compact, waterless formulations. Our focus remains on optimizing solid dosage formats, moisture stability, and infinite-recyclability packaging systems.
