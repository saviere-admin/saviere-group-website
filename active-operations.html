Phase 1 Intervention

The DOTS Protocol.

An anhydrous (zero-water), solid-state toothpaste tablet engineered to replace standard 100g silica paste tubes.

1. Eliminating Plastic Micro-Vectors

Standard oral care relies on abrasive silica gels packaged in single-use plastic tubes, which continuously shed microplastics into the paste. Our intervention removes single-use plastics entirely, completely eliminating the metabolic and carcinogenic risks associated with daily microplastic ingestion.

2. Anhydrous Compression

Traditional toothpaste consists of up to 50% water, generating a massive, unnecessary logistical carbon footprint. Pressed into a precise 600mg format, we compress our active biomimetic chemistry, slashing shipping weight by 70%.

3. Clinical Biomimetics (Nano-HAp)

DOTS utilizes Nano-HAp particles to seamlessly bind to the enamel matrix, filling micro-fissures, reducing dentin hypersensitivity, and reversing early-stage caries without the neurotoxic risks of fluoride ingestion. It is further formulated with non-cariogenic sugar alcohols (Xylitol/Erythritol) that selectively starve Streptococcus mutans, actively preventing pathogenic plaque biofilms.
