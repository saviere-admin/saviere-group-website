Developing

Tooth Dots.

Our first operational focus addresses material waste in daily routines. We are currently developing a waterless, solid-format oral care platform.

The Engineering Context

  • — Conventional oral-care products are transported with substantial water content.
  • — Traditional tube packaging generates ongoing material and waste challenges.
  • — Solid-format oral care provides opportunities for more efficient packaging and logistics.

Current Investigation

Savière is evaluating waterless formulation, solid dosage formats, moisture stability, and packaging efficiency. We prioritize evidence and structural engineering.

Note: Tooth Dots is currently in development.
