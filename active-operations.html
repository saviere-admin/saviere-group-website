The Engineering Context

  • — Conventional oral-care products are transported with substantial water content.
  • — Traditional tube packaging generates ongoing material and waste challenges globally.
  • — Solid-format oral care provides opportunities for more efficient packaging and logistics.
  • — Consumers are increasingly seeking convenient, lower-waste alternatives.

Current Investigation

Savière is currently focused on the development of a waterless oral care solution. Our ongoing research and engineering efforts are evaluating:

Waterless Formulation

Solid Dosage Format

Moisture Stability

Packaging Efficiency

Portability

Manufacturing Scalability

Development Process

Research → Formulate → Test → Validate → Scale
