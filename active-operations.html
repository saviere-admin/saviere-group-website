Phase 1 Operations

The Dots Company.

Replacing plastic silica paste with clinical-grade, zero-water biomimetic Nano-HAp tablets.

1. Anhydrous Formulation

Water is eliminated. Biomimetic Nano-Hydroxyapatite is compressed into precisely dosed 600mg solid-state tablets, slashing shipping emissions by 70%.

2. Zero-Plastic Vaults

To command consumer trust, tablets drop into sustainable, refillable frosted glass vessels, completely bypassing daily microplastic ingestion.

3. Airtight Preservation

An airtight brushed-aluminum lid with a medical-grade silicone gasket seals the environment, ensuring the clinical integrity of the tablets in humid bathrooms.
