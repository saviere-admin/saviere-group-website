Macroeconomic Realities & Market Need

Why India, the Consumer, and the Earth Require a Systemic Industrial Reset.

66M+

Fluorosis Burden

Over 66 million Indians suffer from endemic groundwater fluorosis across 200+ districts. Continuing to sell fluoride-laden oral paste in these zones is clinically unsound.

1.5B+

Tubes Landfilled Yearly

Over 1.5 billion plastic-aluminum laminated tubes enter Indian landfills annually, taking 500+ years to degrade while shedding microplastics into oral biomes.

50%

Water Inefficiency

Standard FMCG pastes consist of up to 50% dead-weight water, creating excessive freight carbon emissions in a water-stressed nation.

70%

Reactive Health Spend

70% of Indian healthcare expenditure is out-of-pocket and reactive. Accessible preventative healthcare networks are urgently required.
