The Global Crisis

The Industrial System is Broken.

We refuse to inherit an ecosystem designed to fail. By analyzing macro-environmental and clinical data, we identify the greatest threats to human health and biological preservation.

66M+

Fluorosis Burden

Over 66 million Indians suffer from groundwater fluorosis (Over 200M+ globally). Pushing synthetic fluoride pastes into these zones is a profound clinical failure.

1.5B+

Landfilled Tubes

1.5 billion plastic laminate tubes enter Indian landfills annually (Over 20B+ globally). Taking 500 years to degrade, they leach microplastics directly into marine biomes.

50%

Water Dead-Weight

Standard FMCG pastes consist of up to 50% water (Over 1.5M metric tons transported globally). This creates massive, unnecessary carbon freight emissions.

70%

Reactive Health Spend

70% of Indian healthcare expenditure is purely reactive (Over 50% in emerging markets globally). We are shifting the paradigm toward biological prevention.
