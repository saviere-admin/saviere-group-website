The Global Crisis

The Industrial System is Broken.

We refuse to inherit an ecosystem designed to fail. By analyzing macro-environmental and clinical data, we identify the greatest threats to human biology.

66M+

Fluorosis Burden (India)

According to the Ministry of Health, over 66 million Indians suffer from endemic groundwater fluorosis. Distributing synthetic fluoride in these corridors is a profound clinical failure.

80%

Microplastic Ingestion

A 2022 study published in Environment International detected microplastics in 80% of human blood samples tested. The degradation of single-use consumer packaging is a direct vector.

1.5B+

Landfilled Laminate Tubes

Over 1.5 billion non-recyclable multi-laminate tubes enter Indian landfills annually (20 billion globally). They take 500+ years to degrade, leaching directly into our water tables.

50%

Water Dead-Weight

Standard FMCG pastes consist of up to 50% water. Shipping millions of metric tons of water globally creates massive, unnecessary carbon freight emissions.
