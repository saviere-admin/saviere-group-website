Why the Earth requires a medical intervention.

We refuse to inherit an ecosystem built on depletion. By analyzing the intersection of clinical pathology and industrial waste, we target the root causes of global health decay.

Billions

Toxic Vectors

The global oral care industry produces billions of single-use, non-recyclable plastic tubes annually. Daily buccal absorption and gastrointestinal ingestion of these microplastics are clinically linked to severe endocrine disruption, metabolic syndrome, and elevated oncological risks.

Millions

Endemic Fluorosis

Millions of Indians suffer from endemic dental and skeletal fluorosis due to high fluoride levels in groundwater. Continuing to import and distribute synthetic fluoride-based oral care into these regions is clinically irresponsible.

50% Waste

Logistical Exhaustion

Traditional toothpaste consists of up to 50% water. This generates a massive, unnecessary logistical carbon footprint through heavy shipping. In a drought-prone nation like India, this represents a profound waste of a scarce resource.
