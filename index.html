The Global Crisis

The Industrial System is Broken.

We refuse to inherit an ecosystem designed to fail. By analyzing macro-environmental and clinical data, we identify the greatest threats to human biology.

66M+

Fluorosis Burden (India)

According to clinical data, over 66 million Indians suffer from endemic groundwater fluorosis. Distributing synthetic fluoride in these corridors is a profound failure.

80%

Microplastic Ingestion

Recent studies detected microplastics in 80% of human blood samples tested. The degradation of single-use consumer packaging is a direct, systemic vector.

1.5B+

Landfilled Plastic Tubes

Over 1.5 billion non-recyclable tubes enter Indian landfills annually (20 billion globally). Taking 500+ years to degrade, they leach directly into our water tables.

50%

Water Dead-Weight

Standard FMCG products consist of up to 50% water. Shipping millions of metric tons of water globally creates massive, unnecessary carbon freight emissions.
