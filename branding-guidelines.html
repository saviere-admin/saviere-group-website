Typography Hierarchy

Marcellus (The Seasons)

Application: Logos, Main Document Titles, `H1` and `H2` Headings.
Psychology: Projects luxury, institutional warmth, and multi-decade legacy.

Poppins

Application: Navigation bars, Action Buttons, Eyebrow text (tiny uppercase tags above headers).
Psychology: Geometric, modern, friendly balance to the heavy serif.

Nunito Sans (Avenir Alternative)

Application: All long-form paragraphs, email signatures, legal B2B contracts.
Psychology: Flawless mobile/digital readability and clean data presentation.
