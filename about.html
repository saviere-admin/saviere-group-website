The Institution.

The Origin

The name Savière serves as an intergenerational anchor. It is derived from the foundational names of the founder's parents—Satish Kumar Ayenampudi and Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi—synthesized with the concept of an era.

It represents a commitment to human wellbeing, responsible enterprise, environmental responsibility, and multi-decade thinking.

Leadership

Savière Group was established by Dr. Tanmai Aasrith Varma Ayenampudi, serving as Founder, Chairman & Managing Director.

Applying a background in medicine to entrepreneurial ambition, the founder's objective is to systematically engineer businesses that prioritize scientific discipline, quality, and institutional permanence over short-term ventures.

How We Think.

Scientific Discipline

Decisions and product claims are guided by evidence. We prioritize empirical validation over marketing rhetoric.

Capital Discipline

We build with a focus on sustainable unit economics, careful resource allocation, and organic resilience.

Institutional Vision

Savière is intended to become an enduring institution rather than a collection of disparate, temporary ventures.
