The Origin Story

Sa • Vi • Ère — An Era Built on Generational Resilience.

The name Savière is a sacred synthesis of foundational roots. It merges the names of the founder's parents—Satish Kumar Ayenampudi and Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi—with the French concept of Ère (Era). It represents an unyielding generational era of integrity, compassion, and tireless service.

From their enduring mentorship and selfless sacrifices, the foundation of Savière was instilled with an absolute moral imperative: that any business built under this name must uplift human health, practice non-violence toward all sentient beings, and leave the planet cleaner than we inherited it.

The iconic Swan emblem abstracts the Telugu scripts స (Sa) and వి (Vi) into graceful, fluid currents—symbolizing biological purity, clean energy circuits, and the graceful strength of a continuous legacy.

Etymology of Savière

Sa (స) Satish Kumar Ayenampudi — Resilience, Discipline, Unwavering Support
Vi (వి) Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi — Compassion, Nurturance, Ethical Clarity
