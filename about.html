The Origin Story

Sa • Vi • Ère — An Era Built on Generational Resilience.

The name Savière is not merely a corporate identifier; it is a profound familial anchor. It synthesizes the names of the founder's parents—Satish Kumar Ayenampudi and Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi—with the French concept of Ère (Era).

Their enduring mentorship instilled an absolute moral imperative into this conglomerate: any business built under this name must uplift human health, practice non-violence toward all sentient beings, and actively leave the planet cleaner than we inherited it.

The iconic Swan emblem abstracts the Telugu scripts స (Sa) and వి (Vi) into graceful, fluid currents—symbolizing biological purity and the strength of a continuous legacy.

Etymology

Sa (స) Satish Kumar Ayenampudi — Resilience & Discipline
Vi (వి) Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi — Compassion & Clarity
Ère The Era — A 100-Year Vision for Sustainable Industry
