The Origin

The name Savière serves as an intergenerational anchor. It is derived from the foundational names of the founder's parents—Satish Kumar Ayenampudi and Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi—synthesized with the concept of an era.

It represents a commitment to human wellbeing, responsible enterprise, and multi-decade institution building.

Leadership

Savière Group was established by Dr. Tanmai Aasrith Varma Ayenampudi, serving as Founder, Chairman & Managing Director.

Applying a background in medicine to entrepreneurial ambition, the founder's objective is to systematically engineer businesses that prioritize scientific discipline, quality, and institutional permanence.
