The Origin Story

An Era Built on Resilience.

The name Savière is deeply rooted in a familial anchor. It is a synthesis of the foundational names of the founder's parents—Satish Kumar Ayenampudi and Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi—combined with the French concept of Ère (Era).

This nomenclature established an unyielding moral imperative within the enterprise: any business scaled under this group must uplift human conditions and adhere to strict ethical frameworks.

Etymology

Sa (స) Satish Kumar Ayenampudi — Representing structural resilience and discipline.
Vi (వి) Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi — Representing ethical clarity and strategic compassion.
