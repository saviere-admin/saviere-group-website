The Origin Story

Savière — An Era Built on Generational Resilience.

The name Savière is a profound familial anchor. It synthesizes the names of the founder's parents—Satish Kumar Ayenampudi and Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi—with the French concept of Ère (Era).

Their enduring mentorship instilled an absolute moral imperative: any enterprise built under this name must uplift human health, practice non-violence toward all sentient beings, and actively leave the planet cleaner than we inherited it.

"A society grows great when individuals plant trees in whose shade they shall never sit."

Etymology

Sa (స) Satish Kumar Ayenampudi — Representing unwavering resilience, discipline, and absolute support.
Vi (వి) Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi — Representing deep compassion, nurturance, and ethical clarity.

Dr. Tanmai Aasrith Varma Ayenampudi

Founder, Chairman & Managing Director

As a medical doctor and environmental advocate, Tanmai approaches broken consumer supply chains with diagnostic precision. We intervene where legacy industries have neglected the consumer, replacing harsh, pollutive synthetics with deeply humane, sustainable alternatives.

Furthermore, drawing from the intense physical and mental discipline of long-distance running, Tanmai builds the Savière Group with the pacing, endurance, and stamina required for a multi-decade legacy. This is not a sprint for quarterly margins; it is an ultramarathon for planetary wellness.
