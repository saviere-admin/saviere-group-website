Origins & Foundations

The Origin of Savière.

An emerging enterprise built on familial commitment, scientific rigor, and multi-generational institution building.

A Generational Commitment

The name Savière derives from an intergenerational commitment. It synthesizes the foundational roots of the founder's parents—Satish Kumar Ayenampudi and Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi—with the French concept of Ère (Era).

This foundation anchors the organization's operating values: resilience in execution, compassion in purpose, and a commitment to building enterprises that deliver lasting value over decades.

Etymology

Sa (స) Satish Kumar Ayenampudi — Structural resilience and discipline.
Vi (వి) Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi — Nurturing compassion and ethical clarity.
Ère The Era — A multi-decade horizon for responsible business.

Dr. Tanmai Aasrith Varma Ayenampudi

Founder, Chairman & Managing Director

Savière Group was established by Dr. Tanmai Aasrith Varma Ayenampudi. Trained in medicine, the founder brings diagnostic discipline, evidence-led thinking, and careful problem evaluation to business engineering.

Complementing medical rigor with the stamina and pacing of long-distance running, the group's leadership approaches enterprise building as an endurance discipline: prioritizing strong fundamentals, capital preservation, and quality over short-term quarterly speculation.
