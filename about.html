A Deep Corporate Anchor

The name Savière is deeply rooted in a familial anchor. It masterfully synthesizes the foundational names of the founder's parents—Satish Kumar Ayenampudi and Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi—with the French concept of Ère (Era).

This nomenclature establishes an unyielding moral imperative within the enterprise: any business built or scaled under this group must uplift human conditions, solve genuine systemic problems, and adhere to the strictest ethical frameworks.

The Savière Group is designed to be an institution that easily outlasts its founders, engineering businesses that provide multi-decade value.

Etymology Breakdown

Sa (స) Derived from Satish Kumar Ayenampudi. Represents structural resilience, operational discipline, and unwavering foundational support.
Vi (వి) Derived from Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi. Represents strategic compassion, ethical clarity, and human nurturance.
Ère The French word for Era. Represents our multi-decade horizon for building and sustaining responsible corporate enterprises.
