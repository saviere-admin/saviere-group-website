Sa • Vi • Ère

The name Savière is a profound familial anchor. It synthesizes the names of the founder's parents—Satish Kumar Ayenampudi and Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi—with the French concept of Ère (Era).

Their enduring mentorship instilled an absolute moral imperative: any business built under this name must uplift human health, practice non-violence toward all sentient beings, and actively restore the planet.

The iconic Swan emblem abstracts the Telugu scripts స (Sa) and వి (Vi) into graceful, fluid currents—symbolizing biological purity and the strength of a continuous legacy.
