Origins & Foundations

The Origin of Savière.

An emerging enterprise built on generational commitment, scientific discipline, and multi-generational institution building.

A Generational Commitment

The name Savière derives from an intergenerational commitment. It synthesizes the foundational roots of the founder's parents—Satish Kumar Ayenampudi and Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi—with the French concept of Ère (Era).

This foundation anchors the organization's operating values: resilience in execution, compassion in purpose, and a commitment to building enterprises that deliver lasting value over decades.

Etymology

Sa (స) Satish Kumar Ayenampudi — Structural resilience and discipline.
Vi (వి) Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi — Nurturing compassion and ethical clarity.
Ère The Era — A multi-decade horizon for responsible business.
