A Deep Corporate Anchor

The name Savière is deeply rooted in a familial anchor. It masterfully synthesizes the foundational names of the founder's parents—Satish Kumar Ayenampudi and Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi—with the French concept of Ère (Era).

This nomenclature establishes an unyielding corporate mandate: any business built, acquired, or scaled under this group must solve genuine systemic problems, adhere to the strictest ethical frameworks, and demonstrate exceptional operational discipline.

Etymology Breakdown

Sa (స) Derived from Satish Kumar Ayenampudi. Represents structural resilience, operational discipline, and unwavering foundational support.
Vi (వి) Derived from Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi. Represents strategic clarity, ethical governance, and human nurturance.
Ère The French word for Era. Represents our multi-decade horizon for building and sustaining responsible corporate enterprises.
