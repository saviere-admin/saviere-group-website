The Origin Story

An Era Built on Generational Resilience.

The name Savière is not merely a corporate identifier; it is a profound familial anchor. It synthesizes the names of the founder's parents—Satish Kumar Ayenampudi and Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi—with the French concept of Ère (Era).

Their enduring mentorship instilled an absolute moral imperative: any enterprise built under this name must uplift human health, practice non-violence toward all sentient beings, and actively leave the planet cleaner than we inherited it.
