The Origin.

The name Savière is rooted in a familial connection. It is a synthesis of the foundational names of the founder's parents—Satish Kumar Ayenampudi and Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi—combined with the French concept of Ère (Era).

This history gives the company its meaning. We are building an enterprise grounded in resilience and compassion, designed to create long-term value for consumers while prioritizing responsible development.

Etymology

Sa (స) Satish Kumar Ayenampudi
Vi (వి) Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi

Leadership

Dr. Tanmai Aasrith Varma Ayenampudi

Founder, Chairman & Managing Director

Savière Group is led by Dr. Tanmai Aasrith Varma Ayenampudi. Applying a medical background to business engineering, the founder's objective is to build an institution focused on scientific discipline, quality, and careful capital allocation.

The vision for Savière is to establish an enduring, multi-generational institution rather than simply a collection of short-term ventures.
