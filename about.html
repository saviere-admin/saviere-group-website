The Origin Story

Sa • Vi • Ère — An Era Built on Generational Resilience.

The name Savière is not merely a corporate identifier; it is a profound familial anchor. It synthesizes the names of the founder's parents—Satish Kumar Ayenampudi and Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi—with the French concept of Ère (Era).

Their enduring mentorship instilled an absolute moral imperative into this conglomerate: any enterprise built under this name must uplift human health, practice non-violence toward all sentient beings, and actively leave the planet cleaner than we inherited it. It is a tribute written in permanent infrastructure.

"A society grows great when individuals plant trees in whose shade they shall never sit."

Etymology

Sa (స) Satish Kumar Ayenampudi — Representing unwavering resilience, discipline, and absolute support.
Vi (వి) Vijaya Kumari Ayenampudi — Representing deep compassion, nurturance, and ethical clarity.

Dr. Tanmai Aasrith Varma Ayenampudi

Founder, Chairman & Managing Director

As a medical doctor by profession and an environmental advocate, Tanmai approaches broken consumer supply chains with an unwavering commitment to systemic health. Savière Group intervenes where legacy industries have neglected the consumer, replacing harsh synthetics with warm, deeply human, and sustainable alternatives.
