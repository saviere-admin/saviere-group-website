Strategic Expansion

The Legacy Roadmap.

Phase 1 (2026 – 2028)

FMCG Engine Initiation

Launch The Dots Company (D2C) to address critical gaps in daily hygiene. We are replacing pollutive synthetics with deeply researched biomimetics. Centralize and secure all intellectual property within Savière Estates (IP Vault).

Phase 2 (2028 – 2032)

Healthcare Expansion

Deploy asset-light Savière Hospitals. With a vast majority of medical spend being purely reactive, we are shifting the paradigm toward proactive, accessible biological diagnostics and preventative clinics.

Phase 3 (2032 – 2040)

Multi-Threaded Verticals

Expand into Savière Hotels and Savière Lifestyle (Vegan Cafes / Horology). Redefining living and staying as an experience, fully integrated with Savière Green Energy infrastructure.

Phase 4 (2040+)

Conglomerate Maturation

Scale the portfolio globally. Initiate public offerings or M&A for mature operating companies, and establish the Savière Philanthropic Foundation.
