Clinical Glossary

Nano-Hydroxyapatite (Nano-HAp)

A biocompatible synthetic iteration of the main component of human tooth enamel. It bonds directly to the tooth structure to remineralize microscopic defects without the neurotoxic risks associated with traditional synthetic fluoride.

Endemic Fluorosis

A chronic condition caused by prolonged ingestion of excess fluoride, highly prevalent in Indian groundwater. It leads to severe dental mottling and irreversible skeletal deformation.

Anhydrous Formulation

A product engineered entirely without water. By removing dead-weight liquid, we bypass the need for chemical preservatives (parabens) and dramatically reduce the carbon emissions required for global logistics.
