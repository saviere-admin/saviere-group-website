Strategic Expansion

The Legacy Roadmap.

A disciplined, phased approach to deploying capital and scaling asset-light infrastructure globally.

Phase 1 (2026 – 2028)

FMCG Engine Initiation

Launch our flagship D2C intervention to address critical gaps in daily hygiene. We are replacing pollutive synthetics with deeply researched biomimetics, while centralizing intellectual property within the Savière IP Vault.

Phase 2 (2028 – 2032)

Healthcare Expansion

Deploy asset-light Savière Hospitals. With a vast majority of medical spend being purely reactive, we are shifting the paradigm toward proactive, accessible biological diagnostics and preventative clinics.

Phase 3 (2032 – 2040)

Multi-Threaded Verticals

Expand into Savière Hotels and Lifestyle experiences. Redefining luxury through 100% plant-based culinary programs, fully integrated with Savière Green Energy infrastructure.

Phase 4 (2040+)

Conglomerate Maturation

Scale the portfolio globally. Initiate public offerings or M&A for mature operating companies, and officially establish the Savière Philanthropic Foundation.
