Directional Strategy.

We intend to compound capability over decades rather than chase growth for its own sake. This roadmap represents our operational sequence.

Phase I

Foundation

Establish corporate architecture, launch the first consumer business, build supply-chain discipline, and learn the customer deeply.

Phase II

Proof

Demonstrate product-market fit, verify repeat purchasing behaviors, improve unit economics, and develop reliable distribution channels.

Phase III

Expansion

Selectively enter adjacent categories, build stronger centralized teams, and develop shared operational group capabilities.

Phase IV

Institution

Establish multiple enduring businesses, develop group-level infrastructure, and execute international expansion where scientifically and economically justified.
