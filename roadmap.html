Strategic Horizon Planning

The 20-Year Legacy Plan.

A disciplined, phased accumulation of asset-light scale designed to systematically build an enduring industrial benchmark.

Phase 1: Foundation & Consumer Health

Consumer FMCG Engine Initiation

Scale The Dots Company through direct-to-consumer and quick-commerce channels, establishing domestic market presence, zero-waste supply chains, and clinically validated formulations.

Phase 2: Healthcare Infrastructure

Savière Hospitals & Preventative Networks

Deploy asset-light outpatient medical centers prioritizing systemic biomarker screening, dental-cardiovascular risk reduction, and accessible proactive wellness.

Phase 3: Experiential Living & Energy

Savière Hotels, Cafes & Green Micro-Grids

Expand into management contracts for zero-carbon, plant-based luxury hospitality (Savière Hotels and Vegan Cafes) alongside decentralized clean energy micro-grids.

Phase 4: Global Consolidation

Transnational Enterprise & Foundation

Consolidate mature operating verticals globally, transition mature boards to professional directorships, and launch the Savière Philanthropic Foundation.
