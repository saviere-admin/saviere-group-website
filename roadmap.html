Directional strategy

Earn the next stage.

This roadmap is a directional sequence, not a promise of dates or predetermined outcomes. The next stage should be earned through evidence, product quality, operational reliability and clear economics.

Phase I · Foundation

Build the base properly.

Establish the corporate and operating foundations, understand target customers and markets, test initial product assumptions and build a reliable process for learning from evidence.

Phase II · Proof

Demonstrate what works.

Seek evidence of product usefulness, customer willingness to return, sound unit economics, dependable fulfilment and a credible route to sustained quality. These are gates to prove, not achievements claimed here.

Phase III · Selective expansion

Scale only the proven parts.

Consider adjacent opportunities when the evidence, people, systems, cash flow and operational capability support them. Expansion should follow demonstrated readiness, not a calendar.

How to read this page

Intent is not a milestone.

Phases describe the order of questions a durable business should resolve. They are not forecasts, launch dates, financial guidance or claims that every milestone is complete. Public updates will be added when specific progress is verified.
