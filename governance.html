Corporate Governance.

Savière Group is committed to building systems that demand integrity. As the group scales, we intend to institutionalize the following governance frameworks to guide our conduct.

01 — Integrity & Anti-Corruption

Savière intends to operate strictly through documented, lawful, and transparent processes. We do not regard informal payments or improper influence as acceptable methods of business.

02 — Scientific Integrity

Our claims should be proportional to available evidence. We intend to rely on verified literature and empirical data to inform our product development and market positioning.

03 — Product Responsibility

Safety, quality, and customer welfare must consistently precede growth metrics. We aim to ensure responsible sourcing and supply chain accountability.

04 — Environmental Responsibility

We intend to thoughtfully integrate environmental considerations into our product design. As our businesses develop, principles such as seeking cruelty-free approaches will inform our operational standards.
