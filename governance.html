Corporate Integrity

Growth without shortcuts.

Savière intends to operate with institutional transparency, scientific rigor, and unequivocal accountability across every current and future business domain.

Our Standard

Institutionalizing Integrity from Inception.

Savière does not regard informal payments or improper influence as part of doing business. We intend to build systems that require employees, suppliers, and partners to operate through transparent, documented, and lawful processes.

Operating Principles

01 — Scientific Rigour

Claims must remain strictly proportional to evidence. We rely on primary scientific literature, empirical validation, and rigorous testing rather than marketing assertions.

02 — Anti-Bribery & Anti-Corruption

Absolute adherence to lawful procurement, documented corporate decision-making, transparent accounting, and full regulatory compliance.

03 — Environmental Responsibility

Environmental considerations—material efficiency, water reduction, and lower-impact logistics—are engineered into product economics from day one.

04 — Product Safety & Welfare

Customer safety and biological welfare strictly precede margin expansion. Where applicable across consumer lines, we seek cruelty-free approaches.
